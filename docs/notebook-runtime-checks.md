# Notebook runtime repairs

The audit found five saved exceptions in the 22 tracked notebooks. Every code cell parsed successfully after IPython's normal input transformation. The [initial exception list](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/initial_saved_errors.csv) records the exact notebook and cell IDs; the [executed checks](../validation/xshooter/Notebook_Runtime_Checks.ipynb) preserve the failing extraction inputs and reruns.

The final [saved-file inventory](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/notebook_checks.csv) covers those 22 notebooks plus the new check notebook: zero syntax errors, saved exceptions or logged tracebacks/errors remain. Existing invalid-escape warnings in older plotting labels are still reported by Python's parser.

| Notebook | Cause | Repair and verification |
|---|---|---|
| ZShooter kilonova | The new 20-day BLUE swath had not settled at the 2,000-iteration native limit. | Increase BLUE's science setting to 4,000. The swath stops at iteration 3,304 with the original tolerance. Limits of 4,000, 8,000 and 16,000 give the same refined spectrum and pixel response. |
| X-shooter kilonova | The 20-day faint UVB native fit stops at iteration 967, but its uncertainty refinement cycles under Newton updates. Increasing the native iteration limit does not help. | After 40 unsuccessful Newton updates, restart from the native profile and continue the original alternating equations, with a bound of 10,000 updates. The mask, regularization and spectral convergence tolerance are unchanged. The regression test checks the resulting response against refitted pixel perturbations. |
| Upstream adoption | MOSAIC lacked `INSTRUME`; replacing its detector image with a spectral table also discarded exposure metadata. | IRDB writes the instrument identity. ScopeSim preserves the actual `DIT`, `NDIT` and `EXPTIME` through conversion. Tests check counts and metadata for image, table and collapsed-table outputs. The actual MOSAIC observation cell now passes the existing strict extension/header checks. |
| Developer science validation | A saved trace-coordinate diagnostic came from an older simulator path that rejected image-plane effects. | Rerunning configuration and that diagnostic with the current stack succeeds for all six channels and 125 orders. No new simulator change was needed. |
| Developer PSF/ADC exploration | The slit-comparison cell ran before the cell defining `input_psf`. | A fresh ordered execution of the complete notebook succeeds. |

The extraction repairs were first checked against the exact saved stochastic frames, then exercised for all three epochs in both original notebooks. All 36 arm reductions and nine stitched products complete. The [saved-product checks](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/science_product_checks.csv) verify finite retained samples and agreement between each saved uncertainty squared and its covariance diagonal. The epoch, channel and instrument-comparison figures were regenerated and visually inspected.

The science reductions reuse their native FITS frames and measured calibration geometry; no new noise realization was substituted. ZShooter's separate host/transient diagnostic was also rerun from its optical expectation images. The other 17 existing notebooks received source-syntax and saved-output inspection, not complete new simulations; the table above specifies the additional executions.

In that BLUE diagnostic, [observed aperture counts](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/blue_count_closure.csv) are 1.001793 times the optical expectation, a 0.86-standard-deviation difference. The [component extractions](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/blue_host_check.csv) recover a median transient/input ratio of .9979 and combined scene/input ratio of 1.0658. The [component plot](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/blue_host_contribution.png) shows the host contribution explicitly.

The [swath comparison](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/faint_swath_convergence.csv) retains iteration counts, elapsed time and the absolute/signed spatial-profile integral. BLUE's ratio is 10.56; UVB's is 2.49. Both profiles contain substantial signed structure. Convergence makes the implemented local covariance calculable; it does not turn these faint profiles into measured illumination profiles or establish a transient detection.

The repaired UVB swath takes about 13 seconds including its native fit and uncertainty refinement. The full 20-day [ZShooter arm reductions](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/zshooter_20day_reduction.csv) take 10--49 seconds per arm; the [X-shooter reductions](../validation/xshooter/data/runtime/xshooter_20day_reduction.csv) take 23--61 seconds per mode/arm. These are reduction timings, separate from optical observation time.

The workspace suite passes all 186 tests, the covariance suite all 17 tests (including the new preserved UVB swath), and MOSAIC all 25 effect tests. The fixes are separate commits on the existing `xshooter` branches: PyReduce `9d9b8fe`, DRP `17e4ae9`, ScopeSim `2780412e`, and IRDB `8be3e2c`. The workspace manifest pins those revisions.

The **faint-mode X-shooter NIR flux calibration remains invalid**: its flat-AB control has large spikes near 2.268 microns and an integrated recovered/input ratio of about 4,692. The runtime fixes do not alter those calibration products. Its regenerated science curves must not be used for sensitivity or flux comparisons. The [operating-point assessment](detector-operating-points.md#reduction-and-verification) retains the affected samples and control plot. It also records the separate ZShooter helper issue that discards the requested fixed MJD when that command key is initially absent; saved frames were not regenerated here.

One additional issue was found in the exploratory `psf_adc` notebook: `apply_roundapp` uses a global offset instead of its argument and returns the circle's bounding rectangle without masking the corners. Its circular-aperture throughput numbers need a separate correction before scientific use. That scratch calculation is outside the extraction and runtime repairs above.
